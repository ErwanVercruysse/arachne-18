# Media provenance

The README uses current v4 geometry throughout:

- `assets/cad-overview.png`, `cad-internals.png`, `cad-top.png`, `cad-service.png`, `cad-leg-top.png` and `cad-horn-detail.png` are VTK renders of the exported v4 CAD meshes. The service view raises the cover for visibility.
- `assets/mujoco-preview.png` and `mujoco-grounded.gif` are frames from the current floating-base MuJoCo/BAM body-height exercise. All six feet remain in support during the capture.
- Earlier `hero-workbench.png` and `hero-studio.png` are AI-assisted presentation artwork of earlier concepts. They are preserved as historical media, not current mechanical references.
- All existing learned walking, jumping, terrain and training-army videos are historical pre-v4 results. They were not regenerated or retrained for this release. The video page labels this distinction.

Rendering commands are in [development.md](development.md). Current CAD images are generated directly from geometry, without image-generation edits.
